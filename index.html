Research contributions and coursework in sensing, embedded electronics and signal processing.

Research / WISER LaboratoryOngoing research

Non-invasive potato plant health monitoring

Developing an ESP32 sensing platform to study plant health through spectral measurements, ambient conditions and soil sensing. My contributions cover hardware design, acquisition firmware and a TinyML classification pipeline.

  • ESP32
  • AS7265x
  • Altium Designer
  • TinyML
Hardware and research contributions
  • Integrated an 18-channel AS7265x spectral sensor, SHT31 temperature and humidity sensor, and STEMMA capacitive soil sensor through multiplexed I2C buses.
  • Designed schematics and PCB layouts in Altium Designer, including a TPS63070 buck-boost converter and BQ24075 Li-ion charging circuit.
  • Developed acquisition firmware and a classification pipeline for spectral indices and the study of physiological stress.

Contributed to research with teams from HUST and Grenoble INP – Phelma, UGA, on early-warning services and tropical cyclone track forecasting. The work contributed to two accepted conference papers.

Coursework / Sensors and measuring instrumentsFebruary 2026

Motor angle acquisition & RS485 telemetry

Designed and assembled a two-layer PCB to acquire Hall encoder pulses from a DC motor and communicate measurements over an RS485 interface.

  • C
  • Altium Designer
  • Hall encoder
  • MAX485
Implementation details
  • Designed 5 V/3.3 V power regulation, the microcontroller circuit, encoder inputs and a MAX485 interface.
  • Fabricated, assembled and debugged the board; programmed interrupt-based pulse counting in C.
  • Tested encoder acquisition and transmission of data over RS485.
Coursework / Measurement engineeringOctober 2025

Ultrasonic water level measurement

Built an Arduino system with an HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor, a 1604 LCD and a threshold alarm. Combined median filtering, outlier rejection and a Kalman filter to reduce measurement fluctuations.

  • Arduino Uno R3
  • Embedded C
  • Kalman filter
  • HC-SR04
Implementation details
  • Connected the LCD in direct 4-bit parallel mode and calculated water level from measured distance and tank height.
  • Implemented a median filter over three samples, outlier detection and a one-dimensional Kalman filter in C.
