About Me

I am a final-year undergraduate student in the High-Quality Engineering Training Program in Vietnam (PFIEV), majoring in Industrial Informatics and Automation at Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST).

Currently, I serve as an undergraduate researcher at the WISER Research Lab. My academic and engineering interests lie at the intersection of Embedded Systems, Internet of Things (IoT), TinyML / Edge AI, PCB Hardware Design, and Industrial Automation. I focus on architecting hardware-software co-designed sensor systems for smart agriculture and reliable industrial control.
