About Me

I am a final-year undergraduate student in the French-Vietnamese High-Quality Engineering Program (PFIEV) at Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST), specializing in Industrial Informatics and Automation.

As an undergraduate researcher at the WISER Research Lab, I focus on bridging custom hardware design and edge intelligence.
